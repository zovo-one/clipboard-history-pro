Clipboard History Pro by Zovo v2.2.7

Clipboard History Pro is up to date. You have 0 clips saved, ready to paste again. Here's what changed in this release.

What's new in 2.2.7

Changes in this release

Fixed

A web page can no longer fill or wipe your history

Some sites could fire bursts of copy events that pushed junk into your list and, on the free plan, trimmed your real clips away. Now each click or copy shortcut saves at most one clip, and fake copy events are ignored.

Fixed

Copies keep saving right after an update

Tabs that were already open used to stop saving what you copied after the extension updated, until you reloaded them. They keep working now, starting with this update.

Fixed

Starred clips are kept when the history fills up

Trimming the oldest clips used to delete starred ones too. Starred and pinned clips now stay until you remove them.

Fixed

A settings panel that does what it says

The notifications switch, the duplicates switch, the theme menu and Export All in Settings didn't do anything. Each control that stays now works, and the ones with nothing behind them are gone.

Improved

Fewer nags

The toolbar icon no longer flashes a PRO badge every time you copy at the free limit, and the 50% countdown banner that covered the bottom of the popup is gone.

Improved

Two fewer permissions

Clipboard History Pro no longer asks for notifications or for reading your clipboard in the background. It never used either.

Fixed

Incognito copies stay private

With incognito allowed, text you copied in a private window used to land in your normal history. Copies made in incognito windows aren't saved now.

Improved

Search is ready when the popup opens

The cursor now starts in the search box, so you can press the shortcut and type straight away. The right-click item is also renamed to Open Clipboard History, which is what it always did.

New

Your own numbers, right on this page

This page counts the clips you have saved and how often you've pasted from your history. It reads them from this browser, and none of them leave it.

New

You choose whether this page opens

The popup's Settings has a new switch. Turn it off and future updates arrive without opening a tab.

For Pro members

Fixed

Your license is checked again, safely

Pro was never re-checked after activation. The check now runs, and only a clear answer from the license server that a key is invalid turns Pro off. An outage or a lost connection never does.

Fixed

Ending Pro no longer empties your history

Dropping back to the free plan could delete everything above 100 clips on the next copy. Now nothing is deleted at once, and each new copy replaces one old clip at a time.

Your saved clips, stars and pins carry over exactly as you left them.

Questions people ask

Where is my clipboard history stored?

In this browser, on your computer. Nothing you copy is uploaded. That also means clearing your browser data or reinstalling the extension clears your history.

Why didn't a copy show up?

Clipboard History Pro saves text you copy or cut yourself on a web page, with the keyboard or the page's own right-click menu. A site's own copy button can write to the clipboard without a copy event, so those aren't caught. Copies in other apps, on browser pages like chrome://, and in incognito windows aren't saved either.

Does Clipboard History Pro send anything to a server?

Your clips never leave your browser, and copies you make in incognito windows aren't saved at all. The one exception is Pro, which checks your license key with Zovo's server, sending only the key and the extension's name. If you uninstall, Chrome opens a short feedback page on zovo.one.

What happens if I stop paying for Pro?

You go back to the free limits, and nothing you've saved is deleted at once. Once you're over 100 clips, each new copy replaces the oldest clip that isn't starred or pinned.

Can I get a refund?

Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time. Lifetime is a one-time payment delivered instantly, so it's final once the key and archive have been sent. If anything is faulty or not as described, it gets fixed or refunded.
