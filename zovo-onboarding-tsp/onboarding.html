Why it asks for clipboard accessTo do its one job, Clipboard History reads what you copy and can write a saved clip back when you pick it. That is the whole feature. Clips are stored locally on your machine only.
Why it runs on every siteYou copy things everywhere, so it has to be present everywhere to catch them. It watches only for copies and never sends what you copy to any server.
